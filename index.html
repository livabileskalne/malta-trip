    Getting around and planning

    The practical side: cars, buses, ferries, weather and the baby.

    Car or no car

    Rent a car for Sunday and for any free day you pick a car trip. Evenings work without one, and parking is hard in Valletta, Mdina and Sliema.

    If the Sliema stay has parking, one rental from Sun 15 covers every car day. If not, book single days from a Sliema agency. Ask for an infant seat and check the agreement allows the Gozo ferry.

    Buses

    Buses have seats and standing room. Priority seats near the front are marked for parents with infants, and the buses are low-floor and described as suitable for pushchairs. I could not confirm a pram rule.

    Popular routes such as Sliema to Mdina and the Ċirkewwa ferry get so full that people stand for nearly an hour, and full buses can skip stops. Board at the first stop, the Sliema ferry stop, to get a seat.

    Ferries and rideshare

    The Sliema to Valletta ferry takes about 10 minutes and runs roughly every 30 minutes. In winter the last departure is around 19:30 Monday to Saturday, and about 18:30 on Sundays.

    Use Bolt, Uber or eCabs after that. Standard taxis often have no infant seat, so pre-book the airport transfer with one.

    Weather and daylight

    • Highs around 20°C, nights 14 to 17°C.
    • About 10 rainy days in November, usually short showers.
    • Sea about 21°C, too cold for most people to swim.
    • Sunset about 16:55, sunrise about 06:38.
    • Strong north-westerly wind is common and affects ferries and the cliffs more than rain does.
